Research & Logic Mapping

Appointed under Ministry of Education (MoE) sponsored project at IIITDM Kurnool.

Nyaya Shaastra for Computer Science Engineers

IIITDM Kurnool · Dr. K.E. Srinivasa Desikan · MoE IKS Project (Ongoing May 2026 – Present)

This academic research maps structural parallels between ancient Indian logic (Nyaya / Vaisesika) and modern computing systems. The work proves that double-sided inference, object classification, and axiomatic verification were formalized thousands of years before modern CS.

Anumana ↔ Modus Ponens

Double-sided logical deduction loops matching formal inference engines.

Vaisesika ↔ OOP

Dravya, Guna, Karma mapped to Object, Attribute, and Method definitions.

Vyapti ↔ DB Invariants

Universal invariable relations matching SQL CHECK & foreign key constraints.

Pancavayava ↔ Hoare Logic

Five-step validation sequence matching pre-condition {P}, code C, and post-condition {Q}.

Pariksha ↔ AI Testing

Triple validation sequence matching pytest, adversarial checks, & formal verification.

Upadhi ↔ Dataset Shift

Identifying hidden conditional biases reflecting distribution shifts in ML models.

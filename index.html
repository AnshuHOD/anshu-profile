Nyaya Shaastra for Computer Science Engineers
IIITDM Kurnool · Dr. K.E. Srinivasa Desikan · MoE IKS Project · NGCN Batch 2025–26
This research demonstrates that every major Computer Science system — AI, Databases, Programming Languages, Formal Verification, and Knowledge Graphs — has a direct structural parallel in Nyaya Shaastra. The work shows that Vaisesika (~600 BC) independently invented OOP, Navya-Nyaya (~1325 AD) invented Type Theory, and Pancavayava reasoning anticipated Hoare Logic by 2100 years.
